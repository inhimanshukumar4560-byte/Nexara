Frequently asked questions about our celestial services

Why do I need my exact birth time?

expand_more

Precision is key in the stars. The birth time determines your Rising Sign (Ascendant) and the specific house placements of planets, ensuring your chart is unique to your specific location and moment of arrival.

Can astrology predict the future?

expand_more

Astrology maps energetic potentials and cycles. While it doesn't dictate fate, it provides a cosmic weather report, helping you navigate life's seasons with intention and clarity.

What if I don't know my birth time?

expand_more

We can perform a 'Rectification' reading where we work backward from major life events to estimate your birth time, or we can use a solar chart for general guidance.

Are readings held in-person?

expand_more

We offer both virtual sessions via encrypted video link and intimate in-person readings at our Sedona sanctuary for a fully immersive experience.
